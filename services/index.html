Services

  1. Creative Direction +− Developing the visual concept and direction of a project.

    Creative direction establishes how a project will be presented and how its visual elements work together. For a music release, that might mean connecting the artist’s imagery, styling and beauty direction. For an editorial or campaign, it means developing a concept that guides the images from the initial references through to the shoot.

    The starting point is the artist, brand or idea—not a predetermined aesthetic. The aim is to create a clear direction that the people involved can work toward.

    Depending on the project, this can involve: concept development, visual research, moodboards, direction for styling and beauty, and on-set creative guidance.

    For: artist imagery, release visuals, music-video concepts, editorials, campaigns and runway collaborations.

  2. Visuals +− Photography and videography for artists, brands and editorial projects.

    Visuals brings photography and videography together to create still and moving images for a project. Lighting, composition, setting and direction of the subject are considered together, whether the brief calls for artist portraits, music-release imagery, a fashion editorial, a campaign or video content.

    Photography and video can be commissioned individually or together. A project can follow an existing creative brief or be developed alongside FASHIONROCKSTAR’s creative direction, styling and beauty services. The intended use of the images and videos helps determine the approach, with formats and deliverables agreed before work begins.

    Depending on the project, this can involve: shoot preparation, lighting, subject direction, photography, video capture, image and footage selection, and agreed post-production.

    For: artist portraits, music-release imagery, press photographs, fashion and beauty editorials, campaigns and promotional videos.

  3. Styling +− Developing looks through clothing, accessories and silhouette.

    Styling considers how clothing communicates character and identity, and how a look works in front of the camera or on the runway. It can support an artist’s visual presentation, interpret a designer’s work or bring a specific editorial concept into focus.

    The approach considers the complete look: proportion, texture, movement, accessories and its relationship to the setting and beauty direction. Styling can focus on one look or a coordinated sequence across a project.

    Depending on the project, this can involve: visual references, outfit development, clothing and accessory selection, fittings, and on-set or runway styling.

    For: artist imagery, music videos, fashion editorials, campaigns and runway presentations.

    View related work ↗ Inquire ↗
  4. Beauty +− Makeup and beauty direction developed for the person and the project.

    Beauty shapes how a face is presented through skin, texture, colour and detail. The direction can be understated, graphic or experimental, depending on the subject and the intended image.

    Makeup is considered in relation to the lighting, clothing and setting rather than as a separate finishing touch. For music and fashion projects, it can help establish a recognizable look or support a particular character, mood or concept.

    Depending on the project, this can involve: makeup references, look development, makeup application and on-set adjustments.

    For: artist portraits, music videos, fashion and beauty editorials, and campaigns.
