01 / INTRODUCTION

FASHIONROCKSTAR is an independent creative platform working across fashion, music and image-making.

Founded by Zavyer Vegiard in Montreal, the platform brings together creative direction, photography, styling and beauty to develop visual identities, editorials and image-led projects.

02 / IDENTITY

A complete visual identity.

Creative direction, photography, styling and beauty are approached as connected disciplines. Rather than treating each element separately, FASHIONROCKSTAR considers how concept, image, clothing, beauty and environment work together to shape a complete visual identity.

The platform moves between fashion and music, working with artists, designers and collaborators across artist imagery, editorials, campaigns, music visuals and runway projects.

03 / APPROACH

Storytelling and the mastery of light.

Storytelling and the mastery of light are central to FASHIONROCKSTAR’s approach to image-making. Light is treated as part of the narrative: defining atmosphere, revealing form and shaping how a subject is perceived.

The visual language is built through contrast, silhouette, texture and beauty. Experimentation plays an important role, with an interest in subversive beauty and imagery that challenges conventional ideas of how people, fashion and identity should be presented.

Rather than applying the same aesthetic to every project, FASHIONROCKSTAR develops a distinct visual world around each subject while maintaining a recognizable point of view across the work.

04 / INDEPENDENT PLATFORM

Space to experiment.

Beyond commissioned work, FASHIONROCKSTAR operates as a space for collaboration, experimentation and independent image-making.

Based in Montreal, the platform brings together emerging and established creatives across fashion, music and visual culture, with particular attention to perspectives and communities that remain underrepresented within traditional fashion spaces.

Independent projects create room to explore ideas outside the limits of a commercial brief and allow new creative relationships, visual experiments and forms of expression to develop.

05 / PUBLICATION

ISSUE 01

The FASHIONROCKSTAR publication extends this approach into print and digital editorial work.

Developed independently, it brings together fashion, photography, beauty and visual experimentation to explore identity, self-expression and the different ways people construct and communicate who they are.

The publication creates space for contributors to develop work with greater creative freedom while documenting the people, perspectives and collaborations surrounding FASHIONROCKSTAR.

ISSUE 01 marks the beginning of this ongoing editorial project.
