ABOUT

FASHIONROCKSTAR collaborates with artists, designers and brands to define how they want to be seen and remembered. Through creative direction, photography, styling and beauty, they turn ideas into images, looks and visual identities with a clear point of view.

Founded by Zavyer Vegiard and based in Montreal, FASHIONROCKSTAR works across music and fashion. Their work spans artist imagery, music-video visuals, fashion editorials, campaigns and runway collaborations.

Whether developing an artist’s visual language or shaping a designer’s campaign, each project begins with understanding its subject, audience and ambitions. Concept, casting, clothing, makeup and setting are considered together, connecting the overall direction to the details of each image.

Experimentation and subversive beauty shape their distinctive, recognizable visual language. The aim is to give each artist, designer or brand a visual presence that feels unmistakably their own.

Alongside commissioned work, FASHIONROCKSTAR develops independent projects and collaborations. Their print and digital magazine brings together fashion editorials, photography and beauty to explore identity and self-expression. With a focus on Montreal’s creative community and underrepresented voices, it gives contributors room to experiment and develop more personal work. The magazine also offers a closer look at the people, ideas and collaborations behind the images.
