PROFILE

FASHIONROCKSTAR creates images, develops looks and shapes visual identities across music and fashion. Founded by Zavyer Vegiard and based in Montreal, it is an independent creative entity working through creative direction, photography, styling and beauty.

Its work includes artist imagery, music-video visuals, fashion editorials, campaigns and runway collaborations. Depending on the project, FASHIONROCKSTAR establishes the overall visual direction or contributes through a specific discipline. Clothing, makeup, setting and photography are considered in relation to the person, the music or the idea being presented.

Music and fashion both shape its perspective. The interest is in how an identity is constructed and communicated: how an artist presents a release, how a designer’s work is interpreted, or how a photograph changes the way someone is seen. Experimentation and unconventional beauty are part of that approach, without requiring every project to follow the same aesthetic.

Alongside commissioned work, FASHIONROCKSTAR develops independent projects and collaborations. Its magazine extends this practice into print.
