ZAVYER VEGIARD IS A MONTRÉAL-BASED CREATIVE DIRECTOR, PHOTOGRAPHER AND STYLIST. THROUGH FASHIONROCKSTAR, ZAVYER DEVELOPS EDITORIALS, ARTIST IMAGERY, CAMPAIGNS AND RUNWAY PROJECTS.

THE WORK BEGINS WITH THE PERSON OR IDEA AT ITS CENTRE. LIGHT, SILHOUETTE, CLOTHING AND BEAUTY SHAPE THE IMAGE TOGETHER, WITH AN INTEREST IN IDENTITY, TRANSFORMATION AND UNCONVENTIONAL BEAUTY.

ALONGSIDE COMMISSIONED PROJECTS, FASHIONROCKSTAR CREATES SPACE FOR INDEPENDENT EXPERIMENTATION AND COLLABORATION WITH MONTRÉAL’S CREATIVE COMMUNITY.
