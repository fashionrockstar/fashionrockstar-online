01 / ABOUT

FASHIONROCKSTAR IS AN INDEPENDENT CREATIVE PLATFORM BUILT AROUND IMAGE, IDENTITY AND CULTURE.

BASED IN MONTRÉAL AND FOUNDED BY ZAVYER VEGIARD, FASHIONROCKSTAR MOVES BETWEEN FASHION AND MUSIC THROUGH CREATIVE DIRECTION, PHOTOGRAPHY, STYLING AND BEAUTY. EACH PROJECT BEGINS WITH A POINT OF VIEW AND DEVELOPS INTO A COMPLETE VISUAL WORLD.

CREATIVE DIRECTION / PHOTOGRAPHY / STYLING / BEAUTY

02 / PRACTICE

WE BUILD WORLDS, NOT JUST IMAGES.

CONCEPT, LIGHT, SILHOUETTE, CLOTHING, BEAUTY AND ENVIRONMENT ARE TREATED AS ONE SYSTEM. THE GOAL IS NOT TO APPLY THE SAME AESTHETIC TO EVERY PROJECT, BUT TO BUILD AN IMAGE LANGUAGE THAT BELONGS TO THE PERSON, STORY OR IDEA IN FRONT OF US.

STORYTELLINGLIGHT / IMAGEIDENTITY

LIGHT IS NOT DECORATION. IT SETS THE PSYCHOLOGY OF THE IMAGE — DEFINING ATMOSPHERE, REVEALING FORM AND SHIFTING HOW A SUBJECT IS SEEN. CONTRAST, TEXTURE AND BEAUTY BECOME TOOLS FOR BUILDING TENSION, CHARACTER AND PRESENCE.

03 / INDEPENDENT

INDEPENDENCE CREATES ROOM FOR RISK.

FASHIONROCKSTAR EXISTS BEYOND COMMISSIONED WORK AS A SPACE FOR COLLABORATION, EXPERIMENTATION AND SELF-INITIATED IMAGE-MAKING.

THAT FREEDOM MAKES SPACE FOR NEW CREATIVE RELATIONSHIPS, UNEXPECTED VISUAL LANGUAGES AND PERSPECTIVES THAT DO NOT ALWAYS FIT INSIDE TRADITIONAL FASHION STRUCTURES.

04 / ISSUE 01
