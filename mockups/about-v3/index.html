01 / ABOUT

FASHIONROCKSTAR IS AN INDEPENDENT CREATIVE PLATFORM WORKING ACROSS FASHION, MUSIC AND IMAGE-MAKING.

FOUNDED BY ZAVYER VEGIARD IN MONTRÉAL, THE PLATFORM BRINGS TOGETHER CREATIVE DIRECTION, PHOTOGRAPHY, STYLING AND BEAUTY TO DEVELOP VISUAL IDENTITIES, EDITORIALS AND IMAGE-LED PROJECTS.

CREATIVE DIRECTION / PHOTOGRAPHY / STYLING / BEAUTY

02 / PRACTICE

A COMPLETE VISUAL IDENTITY.

CREATIVE DIRECTION, PHOTOGRAPHY, STYLING AND BEAUTY ARE APPROACHED AS CONNECTED DISCIPLINES. CONCEPT, IMAGE, CLOTHING, BEAUTY AND ENVIRONMENT WORK TOGETHER TO SHAPE A COMPLETE VISUAL IDENTITY.

STORYTELLING / LIGHT / IMAGE / IDENTITY

STORYTELLING AND THE MASTERY OF LIGHT ARE CENTRAL TO FASHIONROCKSTAR’S APPROACH TO IMAGE-MAKING. LIGHT DEFINES ATMOSPHERE, REVEALS FORM AND SHAPES HOW A SUBJECT IS PERCEIVED.

03 / INDEPENDENT

SPACE TO EXPERIMENT.

BEYOND COMMISSIONED WORK, FASHIONROCKSTAR OPERATES AS A SPACE FOR COLLABORATION, EXPERIMENTATION AND INDEPENDENT IMAGE-MAKING.

INDEPENDENT PROJECTS CREATE ROOM TO EXPLORE IDEAS OUTSIDE THE LIMITS OF A COMMERCIAL BRIEF AND ALLOW NEW CREATIVE RELATIONSHIPS, VISUAL EXPERIMENTS AND FORMS OF EXPRESSION TO DEVELOP.

04 / ISSUE 01

ISSUE 01

THE FASHIONROCKSTAR PUBLICATION EXTENDS THIS APPROACH INTO PRINT AND DIGITAL EDITORIAL WORK. ISSUE 01 MARKS THE BEGINNING OF THIS ONGOING EDITORIAL PROJECT.
